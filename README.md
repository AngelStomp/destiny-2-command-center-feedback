# Destiny 2 Command Center · Feedback

Feedback, bug reports and ideas for **Destiny 2 Command Center**, an unofficial, fan-made Destiny 2 planning hub. Not affiliated with Bungie.

This repository holds no code; it is only for hearing from you.

## Report a problem

Open a **[bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)**. The form asks for:

- **The page:** its address or name (for example "Armory → Postmaster").
- **Your device:** phone or computer, and the browser.
- **What happened,** and what you expected instead.
- **Steps to see it again,** if you know them. A screenshot helps.

**Never send passwords, tokens, cookies or your Bungie sign-in details.** We will never ask for them. Your Destiny progress is read from Bungie after you sign in on the site; nothing here needs it.

## Ideas and questions

Use **[Discussions](../../discussions)** for ideas, questions and general feedback. Please search first; adding a 👍 to an existing idea helps more than a duplicate.

## What happens next

We read every report, check whether it is already known, and track confirmed bugs in our own (private) tracker. We reply on your report when it is confirmed and close it once the fix is released, naming the version.
